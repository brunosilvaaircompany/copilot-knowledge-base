# Viewing Copilot license usage in your enterprise

If you have a Copilot Business or Copilot Enterprise plan, you can view usage information for Copilot in your enterprise. This information includes the number of seats assigned and the total spending for the current billing cycle.


1. In the top-right corner of GitHub Enterprise Server, click your profile picture, then click **Enterprise settings**.


1. At the top of the page, click **{% octicon "credit-card" aria-hidden="true" aria-label="credit-card" %} Billing and licensing**.


1. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click the **Copilot** tab.
