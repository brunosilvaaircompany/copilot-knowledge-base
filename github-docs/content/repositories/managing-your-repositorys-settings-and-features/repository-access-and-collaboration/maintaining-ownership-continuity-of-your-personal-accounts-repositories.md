# Maintaining ownership continuity of your personal account's repositories

## Inviting a successor

The person you invite to be your successor must have a GitHub account.

1. In the upper-right corner of any page on GitHub, click your profile picture, then click **{% octicon "gear" aria-hidden="true" aria-label="gear" %} Settings**.

1. In the left sidebar, click **{% octicon "gear" aria-hidden="true" aria-label="gear" %} Account**.


1. Under "Successor settings", to invite a successor, begin typing a username, full name, or email address, then click their name when it appears.

   ![Screenshot of the "Successor settings" section. The string "octocat" is entered in a search field, and Octocat's profile is listed in a dropdown.](/assets/images/help/settings/settings-invite-successor-search-field.png)

1. Click **Add successor**.
1. If prompted, confirm access to your account on GitHub. For more information, see [Sudo Mode](https://docs.github.com/en/authentication/keeping-your-account-and-data-secure/sudo-mode).


The user you've invited will be listed as "Pending" until they agree to become your successor. For more information about what happens after a successor is appointed, see [About successors](/repositories/creating-and-managing-repositories/access-to-repositories#about-successors).
