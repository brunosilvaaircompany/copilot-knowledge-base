# Deleting your personal account

> [!NOTE]
> If your enterprise manages your account and you sign into GitHub through your company's identity provider (IdP), you cannot delete your account. For more information, see [Enterprise Managed Users](https://docs.github.com/en/admin/concepts/identity-and-access-management/enterprise-managed-users).





> [!NOTE]
> * You should contact an enterprise owner before deleting your account on GitHub Enterprise Server.



## Back up your account data

Before you delete your personal account, make a copy of all repositories, private forks, wikis, issues, and pull requests owned by your account.  For more information, see the following articles:

* [Backing Up A Repository](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/archiving-a-github-repository/backing-up-a-repository).
* [Personal Account Reference](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/reference/personal-account-reference#side-effects-of-account-deletion)

## Deleting your personal account

> [!WARNING]
> Once your personal account has been deleted, GitHub{% elsif ghes %}an enterprise owner cannot restore your content.

1. In the upper-right corner of any page on GitHub, click your profile picture, then click **{% octicon "gear" aria-hidden="true" aria-label="gear" %} Settings**.

1. In the left sidebar, click **{% octicon "gear" aria-hidden="true" aria-label="gear" %} Account**.

1. At the bottom of the Account Settings page, under "Delete account", click **Delete your account**. Before you can delete your personal account:
1. In the "Make sure you want to do this" dialog box, complete the steps to confirm you understand what happens when your account is deleted:
    * In the first field, type your username or email.
    * In the second field, type the phrase from the prompt.
