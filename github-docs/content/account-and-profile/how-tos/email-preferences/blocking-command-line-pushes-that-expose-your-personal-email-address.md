# Blocking command line pushes that expose your personal email address

When you enable this setting, each time you push to GitHub, we’ll check the most recent commit. If the author email on that commit is a private email on your GitHub account, we will block the push and warn you about exposing your private email.

1. In the upper-right corner of any page on GitHub, click your profile picture, then click **{% octicon "gear" aria-hidden="true" aria-label="gear" %} Settings**.

1. In the "Access" section of the sidebar, click **{% octicon "mail" aria-hidden="true" aria-label="mail" %} Emails**.

1. To keep your email address private when performing web-based Git operations, select **Keep my email addresses private**.

1. To keep your email address private in commits you push from the command line, select **Block command line pushes that expose my email**.
