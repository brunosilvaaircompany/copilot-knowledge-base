# Changing your billing cycle

## Who can change their billing cycle

You can change your billing cycle if you have a volume-based plan that supports both monthly and annual billing. The change takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

For more information about volume-based plans, see [Billing Cycles](https://docs.github.com/en/billing/concepts/billing-cycles#billing-cycles-for-volume-based-products).

## Changing your billing cycle

1. In the upper-right corner of any page on GitHub, click your profile picture, then click **{% octicon "gear" aria-hidden="true" aria-label="gear" %} Settings**.

1. In the "Access" section of the sidebar, click **{% octicon "credit-card" aria-hidden="true" aria-label="credit-card" %} Billing & licensing** and then **Licensing** (new platform). Alternatively, click **{% octicon "credit-card" aria-hidden="true" aria-label="credit-card" %} Billing and licensing**, then click **Plans and usage** (original platform).


1. Find the plan you want to update, then select the **Edit** dropdown on the right.
1. Select the option to change your billing cycle, then follow the prompts.
